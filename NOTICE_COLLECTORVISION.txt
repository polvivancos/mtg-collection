CollectorVision integration

This app integrates the CollectorVision card-identification project by HanClinto.
Project source: https://github.com/HanClinto/CollectorVision
License: AGPL-3.0

The MTG Collection Pro scanner loads the official browser scanner assets and worker implementation, applies a small runtime integration patch for confidence-margin reporting, and uses Scryfall only for card metadata/printing enrichment after visual identification.
